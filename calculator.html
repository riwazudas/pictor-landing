Pricing & Fees FAQs

Essential regulatory guidelines regarding visa application payments and fees.

Are Department of Home Affairs fees refundable?

No, government visa application charges (VAC) are non-refundable in almost all cases, regardless of whether your visa application is approved, withdrawn, or refused. This is why thorough preparation is critical.

When do visa fees change?

The Australian Department of Home Affairs typically adjusts visa charges on July 1st of every fiscal year. The calculation configuration on this page is read from a central data model which we update in compliance with official announcements.

What other hidden fees should I budget for?

Depending on your specific occupation, skills assessments can require certified translations, curriculum audits, or academic credential validations. Additionally, biometrics collection, health checks by designated panels, and police records checks in countries you resided in for 12+ months are separate, direct-billing items.
