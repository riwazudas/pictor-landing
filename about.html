Our Vision & Values

Navigating Australian Migration With Precision

At Pictor Services, we understand that migration is a life-defining milestone. Our firm was established on the principles of complete transparency, immigration assistance, and compassionate client advocacy.

With active offices in Melbourne and Kathmandu, our registered migration agents (OMARA) and certified counsellors (QEAC) assist clients through every phase—from preliminary points calculation and course selection to visa lodgements and permanent residency pathways.
