Processing Times FAQs

Essential regulatory guidelines regarding visa application timeframes.

What factors influence visa processing times?

Processing times vary significantly based on whether the application is decision-ready (all checklist items, biometrics, and medicals completed upfront), response times to Requests for Information (RFI), complexity of background checks, and caseload volumes.

What does a 'Decision-Ready' application mean?

A decision-ready application is one submitted with all required documentation pre-attached—such as skills assessments, language results, police clearances, and medical examinations—meaning the case officer can make a decision immediately without issuing an RFI.

Are the listed processing times guaranteed?

No, they are based on statistical distributions published by the Department of Home Affairs showing how long 90% of cases take to process. Actual processing times can vary widely based on government priorities and individual case profiles.
