PR Points FAQs

Essential regulatory guidelines regarding visa application point scores.

What is the minimum points score required to apply?

The minimum score required to submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) is 65 points. However, due to high competition, higher scores are often required to receive an invitation to apply depending on your occupation and state nominated.

How long are points calculator factors valid?

Your age points are locked at the time you receive an invitation to apply (ITA). English language tests are valid for up to 3 years prior to invitation, and skills assessments are generally valid for up to 3 years.

Can I combine work experience points?

Yes, you can claim points for both overseas and Australian work experience. However, the combined points for skilled employment are capped at a maximum of 20 points, which is fully modeled in our calculator.
