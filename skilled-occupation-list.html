Australian Visa Pathways

Skilled Occupation List (SOL)

Discover how your professional qualifications map to Australian visa categories, assessing authorities, and ANZSCO skill standards.

1

What is the SOL?

The Skilled Occupation List (SOL) is the legislative instrument compiled by the Australian Department of Home Affairs (DHA) detailing all professional roles that are in high economic demand across the country.

2

ANZSCO Code Alignment

Every eligible occupation maps to a unique 6-digit Australian and New Zealand Standard Classification of Occupations (ANZSCO) code that dictates the exact job description, duties, and required qualifications.

3

Skills Assessment

To apply for skilled migration, you must obtain a positive skills assessment from the designated assessing authority for your specific ANZSCO code to prove your education and work experience meet Australian standard requirements.

Structure & Classification

Primary Skilled Occupation Lists (MLTSSL, STSOL & CSOL)

The overall Skilled Occupation framework is divided into targeted sub-lists governing eligibility for general skilled, state-nominated, regional, and employer-sponsored visa subclasses.

High Priority

MLTSSL

Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List

Includes high-value skills critical to Australia’s long-term economic strategy. Provides pathways for Subclass 189, Subclass 190, and Subclass 491 visas.

Eligible for: 189, 190, 485, 491, 482
Explore Skilled Visas ➔
State & Regional Focus

STSOL

Short-term Skilled Occupation List

Targeted towards temporary shortages and localized labor market gaps. Mainly provides pathways for state-nominated subclass 190 and regional subclass 491 visas.

Eligible for: 190, 491, 482, 494
Explore Nominated Visas ➔
Skills in Demand & Sponsorship

CSOL

Core Skills Occupation List (replaces ROL)

Developed by Jobs and Skills Australia (JSA) for the Core Skills pathway under the Skills in Demand visa framework, supporting key employer-sponsored and targeted regional pathways.

Eligible for: 482 / SID, 494, 186, 491

Official Immigration Disclaimer: General Skilled Migration subclasses, point parameters, and occupation listings are managed directly by the Department of Home Affairs. Rules change periodically based on state quotas and federal targets. Always cross-verify with official portals.
Student & Graduate Disclaimer: If you are applying for study pathways, your chosen qualification should align with an occupation on the Skilled Occupation List to maximize post-study regional graduate (subclass 485) and permanent residency options.
